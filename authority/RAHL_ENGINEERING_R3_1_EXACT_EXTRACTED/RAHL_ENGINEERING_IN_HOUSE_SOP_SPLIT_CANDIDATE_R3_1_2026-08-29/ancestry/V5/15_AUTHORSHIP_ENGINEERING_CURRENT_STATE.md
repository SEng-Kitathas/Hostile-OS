# 15 — Authorship Engineering Current State

Date: 2026-08-28
Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY / NO_CANONICAL_PROMOTION`

## Research object

Authorship Engineering studies how source representation changes semantic recovery, ownership
recovery, lawful modification, repair convergence, verification, future extension, and human/AI
descendant quality. It is not lint and not aesthetics alone.

## AE-001 bounded result

- V1 cosmetic professionalization improved appearance without fixing deep semantic ownership defects.
- V2 strong conventional senior Python captured most of the quality gain.
- V3 Rahl candidate did not dominate V2 on patch correctness/craft, but independently improved
  semantic honesty/recoverability and exposed additional contract information machine-readably.
- V4 showed that explicitness/centralization can become ceremony and common-mode coherent error.
- V5 showed compression/terse source cannot be dismissed by appearance alone.

Thus Rahl-specific authorship must demonstrate **incremental causal value beyond strong ordinary
senior craft**.

`PROFESSIONAL_APPEARANCE != EXPERT_AUTHORSHIP`

## AE-002 / AE-002B corrected candidate

Do **not** universalize “one operational owner + weakest witness.” Current narrower form:

> Make the operational authority topology explicit. Prefer one logical operational owner where one
> owner suffices. For consequential guarantees, add the narrowest discriminative witness whose
> independence, coverage, false-positive/negative costs, placement, and authority are justified by
> the assurance need.

Important distinctions:
- verification authority != selection authority != veto authority;
- a full recomputation checker can become a shadow decision procedure;
- separate implementation != independent failure information;
- witness correctness != witness coverage;
- same property at different placement yields different operational protection;
- deliberate replicated authority differs from accidental duplicate ownership.

## Coherent authorship and novelty escape

“Same author” means continuation of the project's **engineering grammar**—ownership, failure,
causality, naming discipline, host-language fluency, and evidence style. It does not mean freezing
incumbent abstractions.

When the semantics are genuinely novel, local authorial novelty may be required. Concentrate novelty
where the mechanism is novel; keep ordinary support code unsurprising unless the host/domain gives a
reason otherwise.

`COHERENT_AUTHORSHIP != INCUMBENT_PATTERN_LOCK_IN`

`SEMANTIC_NOVELTY_MAY_REQUIRE_LOCAL_AUTHORIAL_NOVELTY`

## Generative/hereditary quality currentness

The hypothesis that source phenotype shapes future human/AI descendants remains promising, but the
measured effect is relative to the evaluator population:
- model family/version;
- tool/file-access surface;
- context window and prompt regime;
- task distribution;
- human expertise distribution;
- repository state/time.

Therefore generative quality is not an intrinsic timeless badge.

`GENERATIVE_QUALITY_AT_MODEL_M != UNIVERSAL_GENERATIVE_QUALITY`

`AI_AUTHORSHIP_ASSAY_REQUIRES_MODEL_TASK_CONTEXT_CURRENTNESS`

## Research roughness / quarantine

Fast spikes may lawfully trade authorship polish for information speed when they are explicitly
research-only, bounded, and disposable. If they acquire dependents, become examples/training context,
feed canonical code, or approach promotion, they owe the relevant authorship/engineering gate.

`THROWAWAY_LABEL != ACTUALLY_THROWAWAY`

## Authorship-quality axes under study

Semantic/causal/ownership/effect/lifecycle legibility; host fluency; truthful beacons; information
scent; change locality; lawful-change gradient; repair convergence; surprisal alignment; edit-topology
alignment; intentional semantic redundancy; generative/hereditary quality.

`VECTOR_VALUED_QUALITY != ALL_AXES_ALWAYS_ACTIVE`

## Current frontier

Continue consequence/recovery coupling, mutation detectability, and real-code phenotype experiments.
Compare against strong ordinary senior code and vary model/human evaluator populations. Avoid
optimizing for “looks senior,” incumbent-pattern conformity, or current model context limits as if
they were domain laws.

`MODEL_CONTEXT_LIMIT != DOMAIN_BOUNDARY`


## V5 conflict guard — authorship is not a master value

Authorship quality is optimized inside the active engineering obligations. A refactor that is easier to
read but violates a required latency, memory, protocol, safety, compatibility, or other standing
property is not an authorship success for that system.

Where the substrate or consequence forces locally awkward code, make the exception deliberate,
bounded, measured, and documented rather than laundering it through a prettier but weaker abstraction.

`AUTHORSHIP_QUALITY != LICENSE_TO_REGRESS_REQUIRED_RUNTIME_PROPERTIES`
