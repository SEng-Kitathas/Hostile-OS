# 18 — Current Whole-Engineering Doctrine Candidates

Date: 2026-08-28
Status: `PROVISIONAL / THREE_HOSTILE_PASSES / BUILD-FORWARD FOUNDATION / NOT FINAL CONSTITUTION`

## High-convergence center

Engineer the smallest **justified** causal structure that carries the required consequence within the
credible interaction and operating field. Preserve standing obligations, expose authority/effect/
recovery topology, use expert authorship, and match claims to evidence whose measurement channel and
scope are explicit.

“Smallest” applies only after sufficiency is established. The doctrine constrains the admissible region
and exposes tradeoffs; it is not a universal scalarizer.

## Current candidates

1. **Reality precedence + measurement humility.** Intent/requirements define the desired direction;
   reality outranks narrative, while observations remain mediated evidence rather than reality itself.
2. **Composition before invention, without legacy worship.** Search live owners, ancestry, and lawful
   compositions before adding primitives. Incumbency does not create permanent entitlement when an
   explicit transfer or replacement is the smaller causal correction.
3. **Minimum Sufficient Embodiment.** Among designs that satisfy active obligations, minimize incidental
   causal and maintenance burden while preserving the credible trajectory. Several incomparable minima
   can be lawful.
4. **Operating envelope + typed reserve.** Do not design normal operation at the failure edge. Treat
   ~20% as a default planning challenge only where a scalar percentage model is meaningful; qualify
   actual headroom with evidence and keep reserve classes/correlation explicit.
5. **Price option value against carrying and lock-in costs.** Preserve future seams only when their
   expected value—including later migration/irreversibility cost—pays their current complexity cost.
6. **Explicit authority topology.** Prefer singular logical ownership where one owner suffices;
   replicated/partitioned/migration authority must expose reconciliation, currentness, and cutover or
   conflict semantics.
7. **Narrow discriminative witnesses.** For consequential guarantees, use witnesses that answer the
   assurance question without gratuitously rebuilding the whole operation. Independence, coverage,
   false-positive/negative costs, placement, and authority must be argued.
8. **Witness placement and authority are part of the guarantee.** Descriptive, verification, selection,
   veto, monitoring, and audit roles are not interchangeable.
9. **Authorship is an engineering plane.** Semantic honesty, information scent, ownership/effect/
   lifecycle legibility, host/domain fluency, change behavior, and expert craft matter separately from
   executable correctness. Professional appearance alone has no authority.
10. **Coherent authorship preserves novelty escape.** Continue the project's engineering grammar, not
    every incumbent abstraction. Concentrate authorial novelty where semantics are genuinely novel.
11. **Generative quality carries evaluator currentness.** Human/AI descendant quality is relative to
    model/tool version, context, task population, expertise population, repository state, and time.
12. **Source signals carry semantic burden.** Names, types, comments, reports, and tests should not
    imply a stronger current guarantee than the artifact establishes.
13. **Quality is consequence- and obligation-relative.** The immediate request selects emphasis but
    does not erase standing safety, security, durability, compatibility, custody, contractual, or other
    active obligations.
14. **Diversity of failure axes and evidence sources.** Reviewer count, axis diversity, and evidence
    independence are distinct; none substitutes automatically for the others.
15. **Procedure serves explicit obligations.** Procedure must have a live causal job—epistemic,
    safety, attribution, reproducibility, auditability, custody, compliance/fairness, reviewability, or
    continuity—not ceremonial existence.
16. **Exact proof and probabilistic evidence are different claim classes.** Exact inheritance requires
    logical sufficiency; bounded probabilistic claims may rely on calibrated evidence with an explicit
    error model.
17. **Verification preserves evidentiary conditions and completeness.** A verifier should not silently
    contaminate its specimen and must test required membership/completeness where the claim depends on
    a selected set.
18. **Continuity has replay classes and checkpoint scope.** Read occurrence, locator, retained bytes,
    replayability, and as-of-checkpoint freshness are different strengths.
19. **Hostile survival is bounded evidence.** Passing known scars and mutations does not establish
    unknown-failure robustness; renew attack axes when broader claims are made.
20. **Research roughness requires quarantine or requalification.** Disposable spikes may trade polish
    for learning speed, but persistence, dependents, example/training use, or promotion trigger the
    relevant authorship and engineering gates.
21. **UNKNOWN does not require paralysis.** Keep uncertainty explicit while permitting authorized,
    reversible, observable, bounded-consequence actions that buy information. Irreversibility raises
    the qualification burden.
22. **External contracts constrain host-native lowering.** Use host-native representations internally;
    do not let local idiom silently change wire, ABI, schema, persistence, or interchange semantics.
23. **Preserve scar evidence, not dead machinery by default.** Keep the discriminator, regression,
    provenance, or rationale needed to prevent recurrence; obsolete implementation needs a current job
    to remain.
24. **Time as cause is distinct from time as epistemic proxy.** Time may be a real causal/contractual
    variable; elapsed time alone does not establish truth or staleness.
25. **Optimize within the admissible set.** Admissibility constraints and standing obligations define
    admissibility. Defaults and optimization pressures operate inside that region. When lawful choices
    remain Pareto-incomparable, expose the tradeoff rather than fabricating a total order.
