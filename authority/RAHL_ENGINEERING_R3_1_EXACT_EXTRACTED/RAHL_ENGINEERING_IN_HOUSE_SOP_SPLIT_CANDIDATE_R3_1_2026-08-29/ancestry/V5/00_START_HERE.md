## Why V5 exists

V4 survived two broad hostile passes, but the third pass changed predicted engineering actions. The
remaining defects were mostly **rule-composition defects**: individually useful pressures could be
misapplied as peers, defaults could look like invariants, and several slogans still lacked enough
scope to survive awkward engineering worlds.

V5 adds four foundation mechanisms before build-forward:

1. **Doctrine statement classes and conflict resolution.** Invariants/standing obligations,
   qualification rules, defaults, heuristics, triggers, scars, and research candidates no longer share
   one implicit authority level.
2. **Counterexample-tested precedence.** Sufficiency and standing obligations define the admissible
   design region; minimality/authorship/performance/reserve/option value optimize inside it.
3. **Circularity/common-mode audit.** The doctrine may guide investigation but is not evidence for its
   own correctness; verifier and witness agreement are interpreted through explicit trust boundaries.
4. **Terminology/prose control.** Current root-level wording was checked for accidental absolutes,
   misleading beacons, clashing meanings, ornamental jargon, and stronger claims than the evidence
   earns.

V5 also adds explicit corrections for external-contract boundaries, bounded action under uncertainty,
scar preservation without dead-code retention, time as a genuine causal variable, option value under
lock-in, and authorship/runtime-property conflicts.
