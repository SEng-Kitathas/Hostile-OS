# Substrate Profile Activation — Dormant Until Earned

R3 does not carry a first-class empty active profile. It carries a schema for creating one when a real version-bound model/tool/runtime behavior earns it.

Activation requires a concrete observed behavior or deficiency, named substrate/environment/epoch, evidence ancestry, consequence, compensation, burden, and retest trigger.

The historical hot-set test did not show that model releases were the main source of SOP churn in the available lineage.

`PROFILE_SCHEMA != ACTIVE_PROFILE_INSTANCE`
`PROFILE_EMPTY != SUBSTRATE_RISK_ABSENT`
`ARCHITECTURAL_HYGIENE != CHURN_HYPOTHESIS_CONFIRMED`
