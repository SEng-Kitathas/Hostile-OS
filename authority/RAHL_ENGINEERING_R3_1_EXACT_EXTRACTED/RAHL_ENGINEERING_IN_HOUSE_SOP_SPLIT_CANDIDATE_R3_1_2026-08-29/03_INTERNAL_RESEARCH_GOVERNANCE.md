# Internal Research Governance — In-House Only

This file governs how Rahl research is run. It is not engineering-product content and gains no product/foundation authority by living beside the engineering surface.

### G01 — Recover continuity before widening

Inspect available project memory, exact artifacts, lineage, current state, scars, rejected/deferred branches, and frontier before inventing a clean slate.

### G02 — Memory is continuity, not proof

Use remembered state to find evidence and preserve intent; reverify current facts when the decision depends on them.

### G03 — Strip donors for parts

Import mechanisms, constraints, scars, counterexamples, and experiment designs; donor identity or prestige does not transfer authority.

### G04 — Separate exploration from promotion

Research branches, donors, prototypes, and candidate mechanisms do not self-promote into canonical or product authority.

### G05 — Run bounded discriminator-led campaigns

Each pass must answer the discriminator exposed by the prior result; stop at the declared boundary and perform breadth checks.

### G06 — Use challenge cases, counterexamples, removals, and altered variants

Test the strongest claim and plausible rivals. Preserve useful failures as scars after repair.

### G07 — Keep live state separate from forensic chronology

Use a compact current operational surface plus append-preferred history when durable continuity machinery exists.

### G08 — Significant work must survive a fresh instance

Checkpoint intent, authority, lineage, evidence, unknowns, scars, and exact next action when work materially changes state.

### G09 — Process instruments must earn existence and residency

A process tool can work correctly and still be unnecessary. Measure whether it improves the research job it exists to serve.

### G10 — Internal research governance is not product content

Do not ship campaign cadence, reviewer choreography, role labels, or other in-house process rules as engineering-product requirements unless the product itself requires them.

### G11 — AI-assisted work is a system, not a single model

Keep model, prompt/context, tools, persistent state, harness, environment, validators, and operator effects separable when attribution matters.

### G12 — Same-model self-critique is search, not an independent vote

Role/context changes can improve search but do not acquire independent evidence authority by naming.

### G13 — Use current AI capability aggressively but verify externally where consequence matters

Models are useful for search, synthesis, code production, translation, and test generation; promotion-bearing claims still need evidence suited to the consequence.

### G14 — Requalify era-conditioned process burden instead of fossilizing it

When a process guard exists because of a specific substrate behavior, preserve the historical failure and retest current enforcement after meaningful substrate change.

### G15 — Do not prewrite the path through an experiment

Plans may bound the search, but the result of Pass N earns Pass N+1.

### G16 — Use plain language without simplifying mechanisms

Spend vocabulary budget on engineering distinctions, not ornamental phrasing.
