# Classified Engineering Authority Surface — R3.1

This is an operational view of the V5/R6 engineering doctrine candidates. It does **not** turn them into peer laws.

Class meaning and conflict order come from `ancestry/V5/27_DOCTRINE_AUTHORITY_CLASSES_AND_CONFLICT_RESOLUTION.md`.
Each item below keeps its inherited statement classes. `authority_effect: NONE_COMPRESSION_ONLY` means this document adds no authority.

### C01 — Reality precedence + measurement humility

Inherited classes: `ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT, QUALIFICATION_RULE`

Intent/requirements define the desired direction; reality outranks narrative, while observations remain mediated evidence rather than reality itself.

### C02 — Composition before invention, without legacy worship

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, HEURISTIC`

Search live owners, ancestry, and lawful compositions before adding primitives. Incumbency does not create permanent entitlement when an explicit transfer or replacement is the smaller causal correction.

### C03 — Minimum Sufficient Embodiment

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, HEURISTIC, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Among designs that satisfy active obligations, minimize incidental causal and maintenance burden while preserving the credible trajectory. Several incomparable minima can be lawful.

### C04 — Operating envelope + typed reserve

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, TRIGGER, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Do not design normal operation at the failure edge. Treat ~20% as a default planning challenge only where a scalar percentage model is meaningful; qualify actual headroom with evidence and keep reserve classes/correlation explicit.

### C05 — Price option value against carrying and lock-in costs

Inherited classes: `HEURISTIC, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Preserve future seams only when their expected value—including later migration/irreversibility cost—pays their current complexity cost.

### C06 — Explicit authority topology

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, DEFAULT`

Prefer singular logical ownership where one owner suffices; replicated/partitioned/migration authority must expose reconciliation, currentness, and cutover or conflict semantics.

### C07 — Narrow discriminative witnesses

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, QUALIFICATION_RULE, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

For consequential guarantees, use witnesses that answer the assurance question without gratuitously rebuilding the whole operation. Independence, coverage, false-positive/negative costs, placement, and authority must be argued.

### C08 — Witness placement and authority are part of the guarantee

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Descriptive, verification, selection, veto, monitoring, and audit roles are not interchangeable.

### C09 — Authorship is an engineering plane

Inherited classes: `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Semantic honesty, information scent, ownership/effect/ lifecycle legibility, host/domain fluency, change behavior, and expert craft matter separately from executable correctness. Professional appearance alone has no authority.

### C10 — Coherent authorship preserves novelty escape

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Continue the project's engineering grammar, not every incumbent abstraction. Concentrate authorial novelty where semantics are genuinely novel.

### C11 — Generative quality carries evaluator currentness

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Human/AI descendant quality is relative to model/tool version, context, task population, expertise population, repository state, and time.

### C12 — Source signals carry semantic burden

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, DEFAULT`

Names, types, comments, reports, and tests should not imply a stronger current guarantee than the artifact establishes.

### C13 — Quality is consequence- and obligation-relative

Inherited classes: `ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT, HEURISTIC`

The immediate request selects emphasis but does not erase standing safety, security, durability, compatibility, custody, contractual, or other active obligations.

### C14 — Diversity of failure axes and evidence sources

Inherited classes: `HEURISTIC, QUALIFICATION_RULE`

Reviewer count, axis diversity, and evidence independence are distinct; none substitutes automatically for the others.

### C15 — Procedure serves explicit obligations

Inherited classes: `ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT, HEURISTIC`

Procedure must have a live causal job—epistemic, safety, attribution, reproducibility, auditability, custody, compliance/fairness, reviewability, or continuity—not ceremonial existence.

### C16 — Exact proof and probabilistic evidence are different claim classes

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE`

Exact inheritance requires logical sufficiency; bounded probabilistic claims may rely on calibrated evidence with an explicit error model.

### C17 — Verification preserves evidentiary conditions and completeness

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE`

A verifier should not silently contaminate its specimen and must test required membership/completeness where the claim depends on a selected set.

### C18 — Continuity has replay classes and checkpoint scope

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE`

Read occurrence, locator, retained bytes, replayability, and as-of-checkpoint freshness are different strengths.

### C19 — Hostile survival is bounded evidence

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, SCAR`

Passing known scars and mutations does not establish unknown-failure robustness; renew attack axes when broader claims are made.

### C20 — Research roughness requires quarantine or requalification

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, TRIGGER`

Disposable spikes may trade polish for learning speed, but persistence, dependents, example/training use, or promotion trigger the relevant authorship and engineering gates.

### C21 — UNKNOWN does not require paralysis

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, HEURISTIC, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`

Keep uncertainty explicit while permitting authorized, reversible, observable, bounded-consequence actions that buy information. Irreversibility raises the qualification burden.

### C22 — External contracts constrain host-native lowering

Inherited classes: `ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT, DEFAULT`

Use host-native representations internally; do not let local idiom silently change wire, ABI, schema, persistence, or interchange semantics.

### C23 — Preserve scar evidence, not dead machinery by default

Inherited classes: `DEFAULT, SCAR`

Keep the discriminator, regression, provenance, or rationale needed to prevent recurrence; obsolete implementation needs a current job to remain.

### C24 — Time as cause is distinct from time as epistemic proxy

Inherited classes: `QUALIFICATION_RULE, SCAR`

Time may be a real causal/contractual variable; elapsed time alone does not establish truth or staleness.

### C25 — Optimize within the admissible set

Inherited classes: `ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT, HEURISTIC`

Admissibility constraints and standing obligations define admissibility. Defaults and optimization pressures operate inside that region. When lawful choices remain Pareto-incomparable, expose the tradeoff rather than fabricating a total order.
