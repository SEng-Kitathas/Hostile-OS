# Mature-OS blind-comparison maturity gate — 2026-08-30

**Mode:** AUDIT / BUILD-PLAN
**Role:** R1 Conservative Auditor + R3 Evidence Synthesizer
**Status:** GATE DEFINITION; NO OUTSIDE ARCHITECTURE INGESTED BY THIS DOCUMENT

## Purpose

Define when independently derived HOSTILE-OS is mature enough to compare against mature external operating-system architectures without turning those systems into architecture parents after the fact.

## Gate prerequisites

The comparison may open only if all are true:

1. **Independent derivation exists.** C001/C002/C003 and descendants have produced an incumbent architecture candidate without modern-system architecture as design authority.
2. **Whole-workload embodiment exists.** A current reviewer body integrates the adopted mechanism family.
3. **Embodiment is reproducible independently of the research ledger.** `os/`-only build/run/verify passes.
4. **Current burden is measured beyond prose.** Exact bytes/state and at least one bounded reproduction/timing characterization exist.
5. **Working nouns remain defeasible.** `activity/binding/resource` are not protected from split/merge/replacement.
6. **External systems remain quarry/comparison evidence.** Similarity cannot retroactively promote an external noun or mechanism into HOSTILE-OS authority.
7. **Comparison cannot rewrite sealed historical results.** It may reinterpret significance prospectively, never recolor prior outcomes.
8. **New apparent capability gaps must return to hostile testing.** “System X has feature Y” is not sufficient reason to add Y.

## Current gate assessment

As of D64/V2-PARETO01, prerequisites 1-8 are **satisfied at governance level**.

Therefore a bounded blind-comparison phase is now **eligible to open**.

This is not architecture promotion and not permission to copy mature-OS mechanisms.

## Comparison questions

The first comparison should ask only:

- Which responsibilities independently reappear across mature systems despite different nouns?
- Which HOSTILE-OS distinctions correspond to consequences mature systems also have to preserve?
- Which mature-system bundles combine distinctions HOSTILE-OS currently keeps separate?
- Which HOSTILE-OS distinctions appear absent or differently placed in mature systems?
- Where do mature systems carry burden for capabilities HOSTILE-OS has not yet attempted?
- Where does HOSTILE-OS currently rely on a hidden environmental subsidy that mature systems internalize?
- Which apparent similarities are merely naming/implementation coincidences rather than mechanism equivalence?

## Quarantine rules

During comparison:

- no mature-system source may be copied into `os/`;
- no modern noun becomes a primitive by familiarity;
- no external implementation becomes a donor algorithm for the current body;
- findings live under research/comparison and are non-authoritative until separately pressure-tested;
- comparison must distinguish responsibility, mechanism, representation, policy, and compatibility surface;
- HOSTILE-OS remains allowed to be worse, better, or simply incomparable on a dimension;
- “converged independently” is interesting evidence, not proof of optimality;
- “different from mature systems” is a discriminator candidate, not proof HOSTILE-OS is novel or wrong.

## Initial comparison scope

Open a small, deliberately heterogeneous set rather than one presumed parent architecture. Candidate families should include at least:
- a monolithic Unix/Linux lineage;
- a capability/microkernel lineage;
- a message/namespace-oriented lineage;
- a DOS-like or otherwise radically different compatibility lineage where useful.

Selection should be based on architectural disagreement, documentation quality and responsibility coverage—not popularity.

## Stop rule

The first comparison tranche stops after a bounded responsibility matrix is complete.

Before any HOSTILE-OS mechanism changes, produce a reconciliation that labels each finding:
- `CONVERGENCE`;
- `DIVERGENCE`;
- `MISSING_CAPABILITY_PRESSURE`;
- `REPRESENTATION_DIFFERENCE`;
- `POLICY_DIFFERENCE`;
- `COMPATIBILITY_ONLY`;
- `UNKNOWN`.

Only `MISSING_CAPABILITY_PRESSURE` may directly propose a new hostile experiment, and even then the external system supplies the question—not the answer.
