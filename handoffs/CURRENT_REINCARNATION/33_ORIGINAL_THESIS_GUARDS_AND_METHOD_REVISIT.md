## Adopted guard — working nouns are not primitives

The current words `activity`, `binding`, and `resource` are **working compression terms**, not constitutional atoms.

They remain subject to the same hostile law originally applied to `Process`, `Scheduler`, `File`, `Manager`, and `Service`:

> A distinction earns primitive statehood only when collapsing it changes a relevant reachable future.

Therefore future work may lawfully:
- split an `activity` into smaller independently future-relevant relations;
- split a `binding` into applicability/currentness/authority/cursor/mode/lifetime relations;
- split or merge `resource` representations where future-equivalence permits;
- replace these names entirely if a lower grammar is cheaper or clearer.

Repeated use in code, prose, tests, or reviewer interfaces does not itself promote a noun into architecture.
