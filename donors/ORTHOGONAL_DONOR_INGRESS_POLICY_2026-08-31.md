# Orthogonal donor ingress policy — hostile derivation remains primary

Status: **ADOPTED RESEARCH METHOD**
Date: 2026-08-31

## Decision

HOSTILE-OS SHALL continue to generate architecture from first-principles hostile derivation and observed responsibility pressure.

Orthogonal operating-system architectures MAY be imported as adversarial donor material, but only after the local responsibility/seam is stated without donor terminology.

Governing sentence remains:

**Donors supply evidence. Reality supplies authority. Familiar architecture supplies no exemption from proof.**

## Required donor order

For a meaningful open seam:
1. state the responsibility, required future and current failure in HOSTILE-OS terms;
2. preregister the local discriminator or pressure question where appropriate;
3. derive at least one donor-independent candidate or identify that no candidate exists yet;
4. choose a small deliberately orthogonal donor set;
5. extract mechanisms/responsibilities/failure tradeoffs, not architecture nouns;
6. translate donor evidence into HOSTILE-OS consequence language;
7. pressure the incumbent and alternate candidates experimentally;
8. admit only distinctions/mechanisms whose burden is earned locally.

## Anti-contamination rules

- Do not begin a seam with `what does Linux/seL4/Plan 9 do?` unless the task is explicitly comparative research.
- Do not promote Process, Thread, Scheduler, File, Service, capability, port, actor, message, VM, address space, lock, transaction or similar donor nouns merely because a donor makes them coherent.
- A donor mechanism may expose a missing responsibility without earning the donor's decomposition.
- Multiple donors agreeing is evidence of recurring pressure, not proof of optimal architecture.
- A weird donor is not privileged because it is weird; a mainstream donor is not privileged because it is mature.
- Donor research stays in the research plane until a separate adoption decision.

## Recommended orthogonal donor families

Use selectively by seam, not as a compulsory reading list:
- capability/key systems such as KeyKOS/EROS/CapROS and seL4-style authority models;
- exokernel/library-OS work for resource exposure and policy separation;
- L4-family microkernels for mechanism/minimality pressure;
- Barrelfish-style multicore/distributed-state reasoning for C005-class seams;
- Plan 9 / Inferno for namespace and distributed-resource pressure;
- Nemesis for resource-accounting and QoS responsibility pressure;
- Oberon-family systems for minimal integrated design pressure;
- Singularity/typed-channel approaches for isolation/communication assumptions;
- unikernel/library-OS designs for whole-image specialization pressure;
- conventional Unix/Linux/Windows/NT only as mature responsibility reservoirs, never default architecture authority.

The useful question is always: **what future did this donor have to preserve, what mechanism did it pay for, and can HOSTILE-OS preserve that future more cheaply?**
