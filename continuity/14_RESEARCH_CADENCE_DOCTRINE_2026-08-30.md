# HOSTILE-OS research cadence doctrine — 2026-08-30

Status: ADOPTED PROCESS CLARIFICATION AFTER WT01
Trigger: original-thesis continuity audit found a real divergence between the original literal 20-pass campaign cadence and the later post-C003 targeted descendant experiment chain.

## Why this clarification exists

The original campaign law solved a real problem: prevent endless story-building, force bounded discriminator progression, retain scars, and hard-stop for reconciliation.

C001/C002/C003 each used exactly 20 scientific passes and closed correctly.

After C003, the remaining questions became sharply localized engineering/science seams. Forcing every one of them into a fresh 20-pass campaign would create pass-count theater and violate the deeper rule that the research unit should match the actual question.

The later targeted experiments generally preserved the important epistemic laws—preregistration, bounded question, run-local inputs, independent closure, scar retention, authority ceilings, separate adoption—but the method change had not been explicitly reconciled.

This document closes that process drift.

## Two-level research cadence

### Level A — broad HSP campaign

Use an exact **20 scientific-pass campaign with P20 hard stop** when:
- entering a new broad problem domain;
- the live discriminator is expected to recursively expose several qualitatively different unknowns;
- ontology/responsibility discovery is still open-ended;
- the project is comparing multiple mechanism families rather than testing one localized seam.

The original C001/C002/C003 model remains the governing broad-domain form.

### Level B — targeted descendant experiment

Use one preregistered bounded experiment when:
- the parent evidence has already localized one specific seam;
- the experiment has a sharp success/failure discriminator;
- forcing 20 passes would add ceremony rather than independent pressure;
- the result can be adopted/demoted without silently redefining the whole architecture.

Examples include R01, I001 descendants, D64 namespace/currentness experiments, IRQCOUNT01, FR01 and WT01.

## Descendant tranche bound

Prospectively, no more than **5 targeted descendant experiments** may accumulate in one continuous tranche without a mandatory reconciliation checkpoint.

A reconciliation checkpoint is not a science pass. It must:
- restate the current architecture posture;
- list what each descendant actually earned;
- identify contradictions/overlap;
- recheck the original thesis/Commander’s Intent where relevant;
- inspect Pareto burden growth;
- decide whether the next work remains localized or should open a new 20-pass campaign;
- decide whether the embodied research OS should converge to the new adopted baseline;
- update continuity and promotion/demotion state.

A promotion review or embodiment convergence review can satisfy this checkpoint if it covers those requirements.

The number 5 is a governance bound aligned with the original OARR slice discipline, not a scientific constant. Changing it requires an explicit process decision.

## Laws shared by both levels

Both broad campaigns and descendant experiments SHALL preserve:
- question/preregistration before mechanism-changing science execution;
- exact input/provenance closure;
- build/fixture/harness/evaluator failures distinguished from mechanism failure;
- `UNKNOWN` for ambiguous process state;
- scar retention;
- literal-boolean checker fields;
- independent audit/readback where practical;
- result separated from adoption/promotion;
- authority ceiling and demotion triggers;
- no architecture laundering through implementation nouns;
- per-turn durable continuity/publication.

## Retroactive disposition

The post-C003 descendant chain is not invalidated by this clarification. Its results remain governed by their original preregistrations and closure artifacts.

The original-thesis audit plus FR01/WT01 closure serves as the reconciliation checkpoint for the accumulated pre-doctrine descendant chain.

The next targeted-descendant tranche count therefore starts at **0** after this closure.

## Relationship to original doctrine

This is an adaptive clarification of the original anti-drift purpose, not a repeal of the 20-pass campaign rule.

Broad open-ended research still uses 20 passes.
Localized already-earned seams may use bounded descendants.
Neither form may become an unbounded chain.
